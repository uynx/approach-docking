Fly and fight

Click the flight view to capture the mouse. Move the mouse to aim your weapon independently of the ship. Left click or Space fires. Esc releases the mouse. Your own hull can block a shot aimed backward.

W/S thrust forward/back. A/D thrust sideways. R/F thrust up/down. Arrow keys pitch and yaw. Q/E roll. Hold B for opposite thrust to brake your drift. Releasing a key lets you coast. Right mouse lets you look around; V recenters. C toggles a stable camera or one that rotates with the ship.

1 and 2 activate the powers you carry. Fly within 4 m of a supply pod with an empty slot to collect it. Every supply pod also restores 15 kg of fuel. Pods return after 12 seconds.

Spin bugs and tow cables require a clear shot within 90 m. Recovery override helps arrest rotation. A kick adds forward velocity. Decoys imitate a ship; scrap armor absorbs 50 damage.

A laser hit removes 25 hull points before armor. Hits damage nearby thruster groups, and unequal thrust can make your ship veer. Collisions also cause damage. Staying beyond the 180 m arena boundary damages your hull.

Each round lasts two minutes. Destroy your opponent, or have more hull integrity when time expires. Equal integrity is a draw. First to three wins takes the match. A disconnected pilot has ten seconds to reconnect.

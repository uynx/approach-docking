Fly the capsule

W / S forward and back. A / D sideways. R / F up and down.

Arrow keys pitch and yaw. Q / E or comma / period roll. Apply the opposite control to stop moving or turning. Hold Shift for short, gentle thruster pulses.

C changes camera. Right mouse drag looks around. V recenters. T resets practice. Esc closes panels. H opens this help.

The ray starts at your nose and marks the station surface it hits. Point the nose at the port, correct your offset, then approach. Begin braking early.

Capture requires a closing speed below 0.15 m/s, sideways speed below 0.05 m/s, offset below 0.25 m, alignment below 3° and rotation below 0.5°/s.

For multiplayer, create a room, share its link, and both press Ready to fly. Dock for up to 1,000 points. Precision, fuel and time affect your score.

FLIGHT MANUAL

How to play

  1. Choose Practice flight to learn the controls, or create a room and send its invite link to a friend.
  2. In a room, both players press Ready to fly. Each ship starts 25 metres from its own port, already lined up.
  3. Reach the glowing cross slowly. A successful dock beats a failed approach. If both players dock, the higher score wins. You have 15 minutes.

Try W, then S

Hold W for one second, then release it. The ship keeps moving. Hold S until the closing speed is near zero, then release it. Holding S longer makes you move away from the station.

Your ship keeps moving when you release a key.

Thrust changes velocity. Opposite thrust slows you down. Rotation also persists: counter-rotate to stop turning. There is no automatic braking or attitude hold.

All capture conditions must be satisfied

  • Within 0.25 m sideways and 0.10 m along the approach axis.
  • Alignment error no more than 3 degrees.
  • Closing speed between 0 and 0.15 m/s; lateral speed no more than 0.05 m/s.
  • Rotation relative to the port no more than 0.5 degrees per second.

Crossing the capture plane outside those limits fails the approach. Flying more than 2 km from the port or exceeding 15 minutes also ends the attempt.

Scoring, out of 1,000

Precision earns up to 600 points, fuel remaining up to 300, and time remaining up to 100. Precision averages four normalized errors: sideways distance / 0.25 m, alignment / 3 degrees, total speed / 0.16 m/s, and rotation rate / 0.5 degrees per second. Smaller errors earn more points. Failed approaches score zero.

Near the port

Keep closing speed below 0.15 m/s. Tap A / D and R / F to correct sideways drift. Hold Shift for gentler thrust. Release Shift when you need full braking. Press C to switch between the docking view and a view behind the ship. Ships do not collide with each other.

Prototype physics: rigid body, ideal thrusters, finite fuel, central Earth gravity and gravity-gradient torque. Capture uses a geometric envelope. Flexible docking hardware, plume interaction, and fuel slosh are not modeled.
